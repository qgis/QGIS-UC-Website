# FAQ

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-6">}}

Answers to common questions about attending the QGIS User Conference 2026. For more detail, see the [Attending](/attending/), [Tickets](/tickets/), [Accommodation](/accommodation/), [Activities](/activities/), and [Apps](/apps/) pages.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## General

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-1">}}

### When and where is the conference?

The main conference takes place on **5–6 October 2026** at **LAAX Galaaxy, Crap Sogn Gion** (2,222 m), above Laax, Switzerland. The Workshop Day follows on **7 October** in Laax Murschetg, and the Contributor Meeting runs **7–9 October**, also in Laax Murschetg.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-2">}}

### What language is the conference held in?

All sessions, talks, and workshops are held in **English**.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-4">}}

### Do I need a ticket?

Yes. **All participants need a ticket**, including speakers and workshop presenters. See the [tickets page](/tickets/) to purchase.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## Tickets & Registration

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-3">}}

### What's the difference between Early Bird and Standard tickets?

None, other than price — Early Bird tickets are simply available in limited numbers before they sell out.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-1">}}

### What's included in my conference ticket?

Your ticket covers the conference itself, the Social Dinner on Monday evening, and the gondola tickets needed to reach the venue. Accommodation is **not included**.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-6">}}

### Do I need to register separately for workshops or other activities?

Yes. The [Workshop Day](/activities/workshops/), [QField Day](/activities/#qfield-day), and the Sunday outdoor activities all require separate registration and have limited places. These can be added when purchasing your conference ticket, or afterwards, subject to availability.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-2">}}

### Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

Tickets can be refunded if cancelled **more than 30 days before the event**, minus the provider's 6% transaction fee. Cancellations made **less than 30 days** before the event are non-refundable.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## Getting There & Getting Around

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-4">}}

### How do I get to Laax?

Most travellers fly into **Zurich Airport**, take the train to **Chur**, then a bus to **Laax Murschetg (Laax Bergbahnen)**. Total travel time is roughly 2.5–3 hours. See the [getting there section](/attending/#getting-there) for train, bus, and car options.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-5">}}

### How do I get up to the venue?

The venue at **Crap Sogn Gion** is reached by **gondola** from Laax Murschetg. Your gondola time is printed on your conference badge, so register for the conference before heading to the base station.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-3">}}

### Is Laax town the same as the venue?

No. **Laax town (Laax Posta)** is a village in the valley, not the venue. The gondola departs from **Laax Murschetg (Laax Bergbahnen)**, a free 5-minute bus ride away.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-1">}}

### Where do I register and collect my badge?

Registration happens in the valley, at **House D in Laax Murschetg** — not at the venue. Desks are open **Sunday 4 October, 18:00–22:00** (alongside the warm-up) and **Monday 5 October, 07:00–08:30**, before the first gondola rides up.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## Accommodation

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-6">}}

### Is accommodation included in my ticket?

No. Participants must **book and pay for their own accommodation**. See the [accommodation page](/accommodation/) for hotels with conference rates and other booking options.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-2">}}

### Where should I stay?

**Laax Murschetg** is recommended since the gondola departs from there, but Laax town and Flims are just a short free bus ride away and often more affordable. Rental apartments are also popular, especially for longer stays.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## Program

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-4">}}

### What's the difference between the conference, the Workshop Day, and the Contributor Meeting?

The **main conference** (talks, keynotes, short workshops) runs **5–6 October** at the venue. The **Workshop Day** (half-day, hands-on workshops) is **7 October** in Laax Murschetg and needs separate registration. The **Contributor Meeting** (hacking on QGIS itself) runs **7–9 October**, also in Laax Murschetg, and is open to anyone who wants to contribute — see [details and registration on GitHub](https://github.com/qgis/QGIS/wiki/29th-Contributor-Meeting-in-Switzerland).

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-3">}}

### Where can I find the full schedule?

The full programme is on the [schedule page](/schedule/), and also in the [official conference app](/apps/#conference-app), which works offline and lets you mark favourite talks.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

## Staying in Touch

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-1">}}

### How will I get updates during the conference?

Registered participants receive an invite to a **Signal group**, used for announcements and schedule changes. Check your spam folder if you don't receive it. See the [apps page](/apps/) for details.

{{< rich-content-end >}}
{{< rich-box-end >}}

{{< rich-box-start >}}
{{< rich-content-start themeClass="coloring-2">}}

### Is there a Code of Conduct?

Yes. The QGIS community [Code of Conduct](https://qgis.org/conduct) applies to all attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, and staff. Report any unacceptable behaviour to event staff or a member of the local committee.

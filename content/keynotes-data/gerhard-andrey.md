Switzerland is discovering open source, not as a niche, but as state policy. A new law makes open source the default for government software, the national E-ID is built on public code, and debates on a government cloud, digital signing of citizens' initiatives and digital sovereignty are reshaping the agenda. This keynote shows how these decisions came about and why open communities like QGIS are key to a sovereign digital future.
